# Default News Anchor · Hotkey P

1. Navigate to **Default Settings → News Anchor Defaults**.
2. Enter **Anchor Name** and **Anchor Title/Role**, then click **Save News Anchor Defaults**.
   "Save All Defaults" also saves this configuration.
3. In **Broadcast Control / Production**, press **P** or click the **P NEWS ANCHOR** button.
4. Preview populates with the default anchor. Pressing **Space / Enter / →** takes or updates the anchor on Program; **←** executes OUT like a normal cue. Pressing **P** again reverts Preview to the selected story cue.
5. **Esc / C** executes OUT. Master logo and ticker remain active.

For field reporters, use **O** and **Field Reporter Defaults**; see [REPORTER_LAPANGAN_DEFAULT.md](REPORTER_LAPANGAN_DEFAULT.md). P and O cannot be active simultaneously in Preview; selecting one dismisses the other.

Hotkey P is supported across all broadcast templates (CNN, Studio themes, Liputan7, and BCC News). Default anchors exist outside rundown cue lists and can be aired without an active rundown.
